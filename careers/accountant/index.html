Accountant

Full-Time / Bengaluru,India /

We are an established infrastructure and real estate firm, Jankal Group, with decades of experience in delivering high-quality projects. We are currently seeking a detail-oriented and dedicated Accountant to join our dynamic team.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Invoice Management: Handle all aspects of invoice processing, verification, and record-keeping.
  • GST Filing: Ensure timely and accurate GST filing and compliance with all regulations.
  • TDS & Professional Tax Filing: Manage TDS deductions and professional tax filing processes.
  • Accounts Payable & Receivable: Monitor and manage payments to vendors, and ensure timely collection from clients.
  • Account Reconciliation: Regularly reconcile accounts to maintain accurate financial records.
  • Tally: Utilize Tally software for data entry, record keeping, and financial reporting.
  • Data Entry: Accurately maintain and update financial information in the system.
  • Banking Work: Handle banking operations, including deposits, withdrawals, and account maintenance.
  • Account Credit Management: Oversee credit control and ensure smooth cash flow management.

Requirements:

  • Proven experience in accounting, particularly in the real estate or infrastructure sector.
  • Strong knowledge of Tally and accounting principles.
  • Hands-on experience with GST, TDS, and Professional Tax Filing.
  • Excellent communication and organizational skills.
  • Proficiency in data entry and management of accounts payable and receivable.

If you're looking for a challenging and rewarding role in a fast-paced environment, apply today and become part of a team that's shaping the future of infrastructure and real estate!

We are looking for a proactive and experienced Accountant who can efficiently manage all financial tasks and coordinate communications with our Chartered Accountant (CA). The ideal candidate will demonstrate ease and efficiency in handling the responsibilities listed above.

If you believe you're the right fit for this role, please send your application to prajwal@jankal.in. We look forward to hearing from you!
